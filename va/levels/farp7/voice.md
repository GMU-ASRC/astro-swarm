# Dr. Blob - Level 7 Tutorial Voice Lines

Record one audio file per line and drop it in this folder as `line_01.mp3`,
`line_02.mp3` and so on (`.wav` and `.ogg` also work). The tutorial plays the file
that matches the line number and stays silent for any line with no recording, so
the lines can be recorded one at a time.

[Line:1]
Two planets today, Commander, and you are responsible for both of them. This one is planet A. There is another one out there called planet B, and I have not put a single defender on it.

[Line:2]
Pick ten or twenty five defenders before you start. All of them are parked here around planet A. That is the whole force. Nothing else is coming.

[Line:3]
Both planets get hit by the same number of evaders, half as many as you have defenders. And a capture takes both ships, so every defender is good for one kill. Leave them all here and planet B falls without a fight, and you get graded on both.

[Line:4]
That gold ship is your shuttle. It carries nothing. Every defender out there is already running the algorithm you wrote in the workspace, and the shuttle reads to them as one of their own, so whatever your when sees ally rule does is how they react to you. That is your steering. It has no vision cone of its own, so it never spots anything for you. Watch what your defenders see instead.

[Line:5]
When you want them to stop reacting to you, press Q. The shuttle goes purple and drops off their sensors completely, so they carry on as if you were never there. Press Q again to light it back up.

[Line:6]
The glowing strip along the edge of the arena is a jump gate, the right edge here at planet A and the left edge at planet B. Fly into it and you come out at the other planet straight away. Defenders that drift into it cross too, but they take a couple of seconds to get there.

[Line:7]
You can lead defenders into the gate yourself, or sit inside the blue band around a planet and press E. That drops a marker where you are, and a defender on the other planet turns purple and flies in through the gate to it. It lands facing the way you were pointing, so line yourself up first.

[Line:8]
You have thirty seconds to split them, or press begin assault when the allocation looks right. Then the evaders come for both planets at once, one at a time onto whichever planet they pick, and the fight goes on at a planet even while you are away from it. The gates stay open and E still calls a defender across, so you can move your line to wherever the evaders are landing. Choose well, Commander.
