# Dr. Blob - Level 8 Tutorial Voice Lines

Record one audio file per line and drop it in this folder as `line_01.mp3`,
`line_02.mp3` and so on (`.wav` and `.ogg` also work). The tutorial plays the file
that matches the line number and stays silent for any line with no recording, so
the lines can be recorded one at a time.

[Line:1]
Three planets this time, Commander. This one is planet A, and it is the hub. Planet B is through the gate on the right edge, and planet C is through the gate on the bottom edge.

[Line:2]
Pick ten, twenty five or fifty defenders before you start. Every one of them starts here around planet A. Planets B and C start empty.

[Line:3]
Each planet gets hit by a third as many evaders as you have defenders, and a capture takes both ships. Every defender is good for one kill, so spread them to where the kills are.

[Line:4]
Your shuttle works the same as before, with no vision cone of its own. Lit, they read it as one of their own and follow your when sees ally rule. Press Q to go dark and slip away without dragging anyone along.

[Line:5]
Fly into a gate and you are through at once. Defenders that drift in follow a couple of seconds later. Planet B sends you back through its left edge, and planet C through its top edge, both straight to planet A.

[Line:6]
Inside the blue band around a planet, press E to call one defender over from whichever other planet holds the most. It flies in through the gate and lands facing the way you were pointing.

[Line:7]
When the window closes, evaders start landing on all three planets at random, and each planet fights with whatever you left there whether you are watching or not. The gates stay open and E still calls a defender across, so keep moving and send help where it is needed. Choose well, Commander.
