# Dr. Blob - Level 8 Tutorial Voice Lines

Record one audio file per line and drop it in this folder as `line_01.mp3`,
`line_02.mp3` and so on (`.wav` and `.ogg` also work). The tutorial plays the file
that matches the line number and stays silent for any line with no recording, so
the lines can be recorded one at a time.

[Line:1]
Two planets today, Commander, and you are responsible for both of them. This one is planet A. There is another one out there called planet B, and I have not put a single defender on it.

[Line:2]
All ten of your defenders are parked here around planet A. That is the whole force. Nothing else is coming.

[Line:3]
Both planets get hit by the same number of evaders. Five each. So ten defenders sitting here means planet B falls without a fight, and you get graded on both.

[Line:4]
That gold ship is your shuttle. It carries nothing. Every defender out there is already running the algorithm you wrote in the workspace, and the shuttle reads to them as one of their own, so whatever your when sees ally rule does is how they react to you. That is your steering.

[Line:5]
When you want them to stop reacting to you, press Q. The shuttle goes purple and drops off their sensors completely, so they carry on as if you were never there. Press Q again to light it back up.

[Line:6]
The blue ring at the edge of the arena is a jump gate. Fly into it and you come out at the other planet. The defenders do not follow you through it.

[Line:7]
To move one across, sit inside the blue band around a planet and press E. That drops a marker where you are, and a defender on the other planet turns purple and flies over to it. It lands facing the way you were pointing, so line yourself up first.

[Line:8]
You have two and a half minutes to split them, or press begin assault when the allocation looks right. Then planet A is attacked, then planet B, both with whatever you left on them. You stay in the cockpit for both fights, so you can still pull a defender onto something it has not seen. Choose well, Commander.
